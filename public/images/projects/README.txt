Place GENUINE project photographs here, one sub-folder per project, for example:

  public/images/projects/maison-tebourba-01/1.jpg
  public/images/projects/maison-tebourba-01/2.jpg
  public/images/projects/maison-tebourba-01/avant.jpg   (before)
  public/images/projects/maison-tebourba-01/apres.jpg   (after)

Then declare the project in data/projects.js (see the instructions at the top of that file).
Recommended: JPEG or WebP, about 1600 px on the long side, under 400 KB each.
Only publish photos you own or have permission to use. Do not add stock photos as "projects".
